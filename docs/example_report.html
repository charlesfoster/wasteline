Unexplained mutations

What this is. High-frequency single-nucleotide mutations in a sample that the demixed lineage mixture does not account for: for each site the frequency expected from the detected lineages and their abundances is compared with the observed frequency. A recurrent mutation (unexplained in 2 or more samples) is an early hint of a novel or cryptic lineage, or of a lineage missing from the barcodes. It is not a variant caller: sequencing errors, mixed-in reference lineages, low depth and amplicon dropout can all produce false hits, so confirm anything interesting on the reads. Thresholds: depth ≥ 20x; alt reads ≥ 10; observed ≥ 5%; excess ≥ 5%; p-value ≤ 1.67e-06.

None found with the current thresholds.

Time series

Mean abundance of the top lineage groups per collection date (4 dates, 2026-01-05 to 2026-02-02); negative controls, undated samples and samples below the coverage cut-off (60% of the genome) are excluded. Several samples on the same date and site are averaged. Viral-load weighted lets each sample count in proportion to its viral load, so a low-load sample moves the curve less; dates without any recorded load are left blank. The pooled panel combines sites by sample count (or by total viral load when weighted).
